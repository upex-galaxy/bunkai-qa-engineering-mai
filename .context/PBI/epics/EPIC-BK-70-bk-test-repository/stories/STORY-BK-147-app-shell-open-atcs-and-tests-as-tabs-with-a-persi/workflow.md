# BK-147 — Workflow

> Jira field: `customfield_10082` · [View in Jira](https://jira.upexgalaxy.com/browse/BK-147)

## User flow

1. Elena opens a project and sees the explorer tree with its modules, ATCs and Tests.
2. She clicks an ATC; it opens as a tab in the workbench while the explorer stays beside it.
3. She clicks a Test; it opens as a second tab. She switches between the two without losing either.
4. From any tab she can reach the project toolbar to create a new ATC or Test, or search.
5. She closes a tab; an adjacent tab becomes active and the explorer remains visible.
6. She shares a direct link to a Test; a teammate opens it and lands on that Test as a tab with the full workbench around it.
